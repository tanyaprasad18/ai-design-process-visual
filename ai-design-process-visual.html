+

Human judgment is the constant across every stage.

AI can organize, draft, synthesize, and accelerate. Designers evaluate evidence, protect participant context, choose the right problem, and make the final experience decisions.

Expected Impact

What to measure

  • Time to first research-plan or questionnaire draft
  • Time from raw feedback to evidence-backed synthesis
  • Requirements and dependencies surfaced before design starts
  • Number of viable concepts explored before commitment

Responsible Use

Guardrails

  • Preserve participant quotes and source links.
  • Do not treat AI summaries as evidence without human review.
  • Use only approved tools for sensitive customer information.
  • Keep final product and experience decisions human-owned.
